# 💫 About Me
Hi there 👋, I'm Zeeshan Majeed, a Backend Engineer based in Stockholm with 5+ years of experience building real-time, event-driven systems, from IoT pipelines processing 10M+ sensor events/day to grid-scale battery platforms.

I currently work in the energy and VPP (virtual power plant) sector at **Greenely**, integrating residential BESS into Nordic grid-balancing markets. Motivated by the clean energy transition and focused on systems that hold up in production.

### 🔋 Recent highlights
- Led the end-to-end integration of new battery brands, scaling the VPP fleet to **4,000+ batteries**
- Re-engineered the mFRR/FCR-D bidding pipeline for hybrid inverters, lifting daily bid volume by **86%**
- Led the re-architecture of the VPP platform from single-brand to multi-brand across **6+ microservices**
- Built battery energy and savings data pipelines powering in-app customer savings reporting

### 🛠️ Previously
- **Northvolt**: event-driven IoT pipelines processing 10M+ sensor events/day across 5+ production lines
- **Valuechecker.ai**: ETL pipelines and FastAPI microservices on AWS
- **Giant Group UK**: .NET microservices for payroll and compliance systems used by 10,000+ users
